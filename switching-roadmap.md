3. Build your "career story"

Instead of saying:

"I know Python, Linux and Docker."

You want something like:

"I support production applications, troubleshoot issues across application and infrastructure layers, automate repetitive support tasks using Python, analyze logs, and work with client teams to resolve incidents."

12. Start applying strategically

Don't apply to everything.

Create three categories.

A — Dream jobs

You meet perhaps 60–70% of requirements.

Apply.

B — Target jobs

You meet 75–90%.

Apply aggressively.

C — Safety jobs

You meet almost everything.

Use these for interview practice and backup offers.
